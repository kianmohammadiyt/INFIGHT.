01 / The fighter is the lab

The first
test is always
the ring.

INFIGHT begins with a boxer, not a boardroom. Every session at Cuban asks the same question: can the equipment under your feet keep up with what the fight demands?

001 is being developed through that question—one movement, one round, one correction at a time. Not a seasonal drop. A first and only answer.

My shoes didn't last, I went through pair after pair . Each session gave me its own problem: ankle pain, slipping, and smudged toenails. I had enough, it was time to take matters into my own hands, and 001 saved me. It will save you too.
